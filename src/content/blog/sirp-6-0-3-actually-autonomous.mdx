Security has had enough “AI-powered” promises.  
We’ve all seen it - flashy dashboards, rule-based “automation,” and chatbots pretending to be analysts.  
That ends with this release.

With **OmniSense** at the core, **SIRP 6.0.3** delivers what everyone else only teased: a **truly autonomous SOC** that detects, learns, and evolves - like your best analyst, but faster, tireless, and a little obsessed with doing the job right.

## **Meet Sara - The Junior Analyst Who Never Sleeps**

She’s not a bot. She’s a brain.

**Sara** is your junior analyst super-nerd - fluent in data, fluent in security, and fluent in you.  
Ask her anything: 

“Show me high-severity phishing incidents from last week.”

She’ll query your database, analyze the trend, and explain why it happened. Assign her a case, and she consults the **OmniSense Core** - a mesh of specialized AI agents - to push it toward resolution.

Sara reasons, correlates, and advises across every domain: data, detection, investigation, even reporting. She’s the new first responder in your SOC - and she gets smarter with every incident.

## **The OmniSense Agentic Mesh - Intelligence in Motion**

Autonomy doesn’t come from one model. It comes from a network of specialists.  
The **OmniSense Mesh** is that network - a living web of agents that think, act, and collaborate.

### **Additions to the One Crew**

*   **Enrichment Agent** - Pulls intel, risk scores, and history the moment an alert lands.
    
*   **Header Analysis Agent** - Decodes email metadata, flags spoofing, hunts phishing.
    
*   **Sandboxing Agent** - Detonates files and URLs to see what’s really inside.
    
*   **IOC Pusher Agent** - Takes what’s bad and makes sure it can’t come back.
    
*   **Pre-Processing Agent** - Cleanses, deduplicates, and declutters before humans even see it.
    
*   **Actions & Tasks Suggestion Agent** - Maps out the playbook in real time. Step by step. Stage by stage.
    

Together, they close the loop - from alert to resolution - no human babysitting required. Phishing? Solved. Context? Enriched. Containment? Triggered. True autonomy in action.

## **A New Kind of Incident Experience**

The interface got brains to match the beauty.  
We rebuilt the entire Incident Management experience for **speed, clarity, and zero friction**.

*   **Unified Activity Dashboard** – Real-time incident trends, peaks, and volume insights.
    
*   **Smart Search & Filtering** – Fuzzy, fast, and typo-proof.
    
*   **One-Click Agent Actions** – Enrich, assign, mitigate, or automate without switching tabs.
    
*   **Insights Panel** – Live SIRP Score, MITRE mapping, collaboration view, related incidents.
    

It’s not just easier to look at.  
It’s easier to _think with._

## **Built on a Smarter Methodology**

We standardized how every incident flows - not by theory, but by lived SOC reality.  
Every action now maps to five concrete stages:

**Triage → Analysis → Mitigate / Contain / Eradicate → Recovery → Comms / Improve**

This lifecycle is baked into everything - from agent logic to Sara’s decision-making.  
It’s how consistency meets autonomy.

## **OmniMap - Your SOC’s Living Memory**

Think of **OmniMap** as your SOC’s cortex.  
It continuously connects the dots across alerts, users, assets, and incidents - revealing how threats move and how your team responds.

*   **Real-Time Graph Mapping** – See every relationship, instantly.
    
*   **Persistent Memory** – Context survives beyond the incident.
    
*   **Interactive Visualization** – Explore paths, impact radius, dependencies.
    
*   **Graph-Backed Decisions** – Every agent action is powered by this map.
    

OmniMap turns data into understanding - and understanding into decisions.

## **Search That Knows What You Meant**

We rebuilt Global Search from scratch. It’s now fast and aware. Partial terms, typos, loose phrasing, it still finds exactly what you need. Because analysts don’t have time to remember syntax. They just need answers.

## **A Refined Identity**

From the login screen to the last pixel, the platform now feels like what it is:  
an **AI-native system built for humans**. Dark gradients, softer contrasts, cleaner layouts - everything engineered for focus and trust.

It’s not just pretty. It’s purposeful.
